**New owner steps: C03-T09, the blind maths teacher pilot (HP-19, OD-24).**

C03's exit needs at least three blind maths teachers to try the function features in paid sessions. The materials are drafted in `docs/design/co-design/c03-teacher-pilot/`: a session script with seven tasks, the information sheet, consent and recruitment message, and a results template. These steps are yours, in order:

1. **Choose the route (OD-24).** (a) Paid usability sessions as product feedback, recommended; or (b) fold the pilot into co-design round 2 (C06-T09). The materials assume (a).
2. **Confirm the university's policy in writing (OD-04).** Ask RV University whether paid product usability sessions with blind teachers need any review. Keep the answer with the project records (not in the public repository if it contains personal data).
3. **Set the budget and payment (OD-10, A11Y-34).** Pick the amount per session, in the plan's band of about $50–85 per hour or the local equivalent, including setup time, for at least three teachers. Pick payment methods a blind teacher can use without sighted help (no inaccessible gift-card portals).
4. **Fill in and check the materials.** Fill in the bracketed items: amount, payment method and time, your contact. Then read the information sheet and consent with NVDA and Narrator and at 200 % zoom before sending them.
5. **Recruit (about six weeks before the sessions, TODO(verify)).** Send the recruitment message through blindness organisations, teachers' networks, and university disability services. Aim for a mix of screen readers. Keep contact details private and outside the repository.
6. **Prepare the build.** Wait until C03-T08 (the function input and calculator) has shipped, Gate U is open and HP-22's NVDA and Narrator checks of those screens pass. Give each teacher an installer, and do a short setup call if they want one.
7. **Run the sessions** with the script, one teacher at a time, remote, on their own screen reader. Record only if they agreed, and keep recordings private.
8. **Pay each teacher** as agreed, whether or not they finished every task.
9. **Write up.** Fill in one anonymised record per session and the aggregate summary from the template. Ask the CURVASONA session (or the coordinator) to log the findings (`F-###`) and triage them into issues. Tell the teachers who asked what changed.

Nothing from these sessions is published as research data (OD-04).
