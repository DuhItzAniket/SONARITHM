## Recruitment message

> **Paid feedback session for blind maths teachers: hearing functions with SONARITHM**
>
> SONARITHM is free, open-source software that turns charts, data and mathematical functions into sound, made for blind and low-vision people first. We are looking for blind maths teachers to try its new function features in one remote session of about 90 minutes: typing a function, finding its zeros, highest and lowest points and asymptotes by listening, and reading the same points as a list.
>
> - You use your own computer and screen reader (Windows with NVDA, JAWS or Narrator; other systems: please ask).
> - You are paid `[amount]` for the session, by a method you choose and can use without sighted help.
> - This is feedback to improve the software, not a research study. Nothing you say is published with your name.
>
> To take part, or to ask a question, contact `[owner contact]`.

## Information sheet

**What this is.** SONARITHM plays graphs and functions as sound. This session asks you, as a blind maths teacher, to try its function features and tell us what works and what does not. It is product feedback to improve the software, not a research study: we will not publish what you say or do as research data.

**What happens.** One remote call of about 90 minutes, with breaks whenever you want. You install the test version of SONARITHM beforehand (we will help). In the session you try a few tasks, such as entering a function and finding where it crosses zero, while thinking aloud, and then we talk about what you would need in a lesson. We are testing the software, not you.

**Payment.** You are paid `[amount]` for the session, including setup time, by `[a method you choose]`, within `[number of days]`. If you stop early, skip tasks or withdraw, you are still paid for the time you spent.

**Recording.** Only if you agree, separately from taking part. You can take part without being recorded. If you agree, the recording is kept privately by the session facilitator, not in the public project, and deleted when the notes are written, unless you agree to something else.

**What we keep.** Written notes of what happened, under a code such as "P2", never your name. They become anonymous findings about the software, such as which sounds were hard to tell apart, and changes to it. Facts about participants, such as which screen reader they use, are only ever reported together for the whole group. Your contact details are used only to arrange the session and the payment, kept privately by the facilitator, and deleted afterwards unless you ask to hear about the changes.

**Your choices.** Taking part is up to you. You can stop at any time, skip any task or question, and withdraw afterwards: tell us, and we will delete your notes before they become findings. None of this affects your payment for the time you spent.

**Who to contact.** `[owner name or role]`, `[owner contact]`.

## Consent

Read each item to the teacher, or send it in their chosen format, and record the answer to each. Verbal consent is recorded by the facilitator writing down the date and the answers under the participant code; it does not need a signature.

1. I have read or heard the information sheet, and my questions were answered. **Yes / No**
2. I agree to take part in a product feedback session about SONARITHM's function features. **Yes / No**
3. I understand this is not a research study and that nothing is published with my name. **Yes / No**
4. I understand that I can stop, skip or withdraw at any time and still be paid for my time. **Yes / No**
5. I agree to the session being recorded, kept privately, and deleted after the notes are written. **Yes / No** (optional; "No" does not affect taking part)
6. I would like to hear what changed because of my feedback. **Yes / No** (optional)

Record: participant code, date, the answer to each item, the format the sheet was given in, and how consent was given (written or verbal). Do not record the person's name in the project files.
