### Findings and triage

Each finding goes to the [findings log](../../../research-paper/findings-log.md) as a product finding (`F-###`, from CURVASONA's range F-200 to F-209), stating that it comes from usability sessions, not a study (OD-04), with counts only. Then:

| Finding | Issue | Severity | Owner of the fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-`[###]` | #`[n]` | | |

Sound changes go through [sound design](../../sound-design.md)'s change control, and keymap or announcement changes through [keyboard and navigation](../../keyboard-and-navigation.md); the HP-21 listening check of the function cues can use the "sounds" notes.
