## What was done

- **C03-T01:** the `sonarithm-curvasona` crate (not published until the facade uses it, C03-T07). The `expr` facade parses the expression itself and lowers it to a fully parenthesized exmex form; exmex 0.21 evaluates and differentiates it. The `function` source samples the domain into `x`, `y`, `dy`, every row `synthetic`. Codes `SNR-ENG-400` to `406`; fuzz target `expr_parse` (nightly); licence check of exmex and rubato in supply-chain-and-licensing; evalexpr was already banned in `deny.toml`. `sonarithm_data::SourceKind::Function` added.

- **C03-T02:** `scan` finds poles, holes, jumps, undefined spans and off-screen spans from the expression's guards, and the 40-curve suite (`suite`) checks it. Code `SNR-ENG-407`.

## Decisions

| Decision | Why | Record |
|---|---|---|
| The `expr` language is specified by an ADR, not only in 07 | What `function.expr` accepts is part of the spec language (AGENTS.md rule 11); the key exists, so an ADR specifies it instead of an RFC adding it (coordinator) | [ADR-0028](../../decisions/0028-function-expression-language.md), accepted 2026-10-31 |
| CURVASONA parses expressions itself and gives exmex only a fully parenthesized form | exmex binds unary minus tighter than `^` (`-2^2` is 4), its errors are not localizable, and the sandbox needs a closed set of names and depth limits before any third-party parser sees the text | [F-190](../../research-paper/findings-log.md#f-190--exmex-alone-reads--22-as-4-so-curvasona-parses-expressions-itself); 07 CURVASONA "As built (C03-T01)" |
| `log` and `sign` are not in the language | `log` means ln in some books and log10 in others; exmex's `signum` gives sign(0) = 1 | 07 CURVASONA; `SNR-ENG-401` help |
| `dy` falls back to central differences where exmex has no derivative | `abs`, `floor`, `min`, `cbrt` and `atan2` have none in exmex; a numerical `dy` is accurate where the function is smooth, and C03-T02/T03 handle the kinks and jumps | `SNR-ENG-405` (Info) |
| Singular points come from the expression's guards, not from denser sampling | The language is closed, so every discontinuity sits at a zero of a known sub-expression; finding those zeros cannot miss a pole between samples, while denser rows would change the meaning of `samples` and the pace | 07 CURVASONA "As built (C03-T02)" |
| An empty row at each pole or hole between samples | Without it a glide sweeps from +∞ to −∞ across a pole | 07 CURVASONA |
| The crate is `publish = false` for now | A new crate's first publish is the owner's (trusted publishing), and nothing published depends on it until C03-T07 | crate `Cargo.toml` |
